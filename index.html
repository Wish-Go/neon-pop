NEON POP

Match three of a colour. Then get out of the way.

Three blocks at a time.

A trio falls. Slide it, spin the colours, drop it. Line up three of the same and they go — then whatever was stacked above falls into the gap, and that can go too.

Match three

Across, down, or diagonally. Four or more scores harder.

Chain it

A clear that causes another clear keeps going. Long chains are where the score is.

Two ways to play

ARCADE for a clean run. FLOW adds items and a second chance.

Built for one hand.

Tap to rotate, slide to move, swipe down to drop. Nothing else to learn. Plays fine on a train, in a queue, or for four minutes before something else.
